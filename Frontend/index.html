About MyBusStand

🚌 Our Mission

MyBusStand is a smart bus travel companion designed to make public transportation accessible, reliable, and stress-free across Tamil Nadu. We connect passengers with real-time bus information, live tracking, and instant route search.


Founded with the goal of digitizing the bus travel experience, we serve thousands of daily commuters across routes from Chennai to Thanjavur and beyond.

⭐ Why Choose Us

  • Real-time live bus tracking
  • 500+ routes across Tamil Nadu
  • Instant fare & seat availability
  • AI-powered chatbot assistance
  • Smart route suggestions
  • 24/7 customer support
