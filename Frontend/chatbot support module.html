Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a bus ticket? +
Simply search for your route, select a bus, and click on the booking option. You can pay online or choose cash on arrival.
What are the payment options? +
We accept credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and digital wallets. You can also pay cash at the bus stand.
How can I track my bus? +
After booking, click on the "Track Bus" button to see real-time location and estimated arrival time.
